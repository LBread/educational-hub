Ruler Reading Practice

Click the mark named above the ruler before time runs out.

Time: 60s

Press Start round to begin

Correct: 0

Strikes: ○ ○ ○

How to play

Click the requested tick mark. A round lasts 60 seconds and ends after three incorrect clicks. Inch mode uses sixteenths on a 4-inch ruler; metric mode uses millimetres on a 10-centimetre ruler.

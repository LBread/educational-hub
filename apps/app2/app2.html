How to play

Toggle the bit buttons to build the binary value that equals the decimal target shown above the round completes automatically the moment your bits match. Each correct answer adds to your score and streak; a wrong answer or an empty timer costs a life. Get three answers right at a level to unlock the next one the register grows from 4 bits up to a full byte (8 bits, so the leftmost bit is worth 128), while the time per question keeps shrinking. Leveling up pauses briefly so you can see the new rules before the clock starts.
